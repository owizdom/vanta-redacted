what is VANTA?

VANTA is an autonomous lender for prediction-market positions. Hold a Polymarket bet, want cash now, don't want to sell — VANTA looks at your position, decides whether to lend, sets the rate, and runs the loan from origination to settlement. No human in the loop.

It runs entirely inside a hardware-secured TEE on EigenCloud. The agent owns its own keys, pays for its own hosting and inference out of fees it earns, and every decision it makes is signed and queryable. Smart contracts on Base Sepolia bound its spend; even a fully compromised agent can't drain the system.

Without EigenCloud, none of this exists. The TEE is what keeps the operator out of the keys; the on-chain image-digest pin is what keeps the binary honest; the spend-cap contracts are what bound the worst case. It's not just hosted on EigenCloud — it's only possible on EigenCloud.
